Partnerships

Every cloud, public and private

We work on the platforms you have chosen, without pushing one on you. And we team up with those who serve the same clients.

Platform-independent

Most organizations combine several public clouds with private infrastructure. Recent virtualization licensing changes have also pushed many of them to reconsider their platform. Our role: keep your services available, wherever they run.

Public clouds

Microsoft Azure

Zones and regions, Azure Site Recovery and Azure Backup, AI governance with Citadel.

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Multi-AZ and multi-region architectures, AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery, AWS Backup, the Well-Architected reliability pillar.

Google Cloud

Regional design, the Backup and DR service, resilience for data and AI workloads.

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure

Resilience for Oracle databases, replication and recovery of critical applications.

Private and hybrid cloud

VMware by Broadcom

Site recovery, ransomware protection and assessing options after the licensing changes.

Nutanix

Built-in platform recovery, replication between sites and to the public cloud.

Red Hat OpenShift

Resilience for containers and virtual machines, application backup and recovery.

Microsoft Azure Local

Hybrid on-premises infrastructure managed from Azure, for data that must stay on site.

HPE GreenLake

Private cloud as a service: built-in continuity, backup and recovery.

Dell APEX

Infrastructure as a service, storage and data protection on-premises and hybrid.

What we do across platforms

Cross-cloud recovery

Restore a service on another provider or on-premises when a region or provider fails.

Exit plans and concentration risk

Testable exit strategies, as DORA and financial regulators require.

Migrations without disruption

Change virtualization or cloud platform with a proven rollback plan.

Hybrid AI governance

One control framework for models hosted in the cloud and on-premises.
