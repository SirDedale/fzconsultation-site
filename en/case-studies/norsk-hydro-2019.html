Case study · Manufacturing

Norsk Hydro: producing by hand rather than paying

Norway, 2019. Ransomware hits the whole group; the company refuses to pay, switches to manual operations and communicates openly.

Sector
Aluminium and energy
Country
Norway, 40 countries affected
Date
March 2019
Estimated cost
NOK 650–750m (about $70m)
Ransom
Not paid

Analysis based solely on public information. FZ Consultation was not involved in this incident and has no affiliation with the organizations named.

What happened

  1. Before the attack

    According to a public analysis, the attackers get in through a phishing email sent from a spoofed customer account.

  2. 19 March 2019

    LockerGoga ransomware encrypts thousands of servers and PCs. Some production lines stop, others switch to manual operation.

  3. First days

    Staff work with pen and paper; retirees come back to help with paper-based procedures.

  4. Following weeks

    The company rebuilds from backups and publishes daily updates. The Extruded Solutions division is hit hardest.

  5. 2020

    The annual report puts the total cost at NOK 650–750m.

What went wrong

According to the public sources listed below.

Spread through the directory

Public analyses indicate the attackers gained domain admin rights and used Active Directory to spread.

Operations that depended on IT

Without order books and production schedules, the extrusion plants could not ship.

Lessons for your organization

  1. 1

    A ready-to-use manual mode

    Document and rehearse the degraded procedures that keep production going.

    Business continuity
  2. 2

    Protect and be able to rebuild the directory

    Privileged accounts and Active Directory recovery deserve a dedicated plan.

    Disaster recovery
  3. 3

    Transparency as a strategy

    Prepared, regular communication preserves the trust of customers and markets.

    Exercises and testing
