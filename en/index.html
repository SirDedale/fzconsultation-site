How we work

A simple cycle, repeated until recovery becomes routine rather than improvisation.

  1. Understand

    Interviews, inventory and impact analysis to set your priorities.

  2. Plan

    Recovery strategy, security controls and clear procedures.

  3. Test

    Exercises and real tests to measure the gap to your targets.

  4. Improve

    Prioritized fixes and follow-up until the next cycle.
