What we do

Our methodology

A simple cycle, repeated until recovery becomes routine rather than improvisation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Interviews, inventory and impact analysis to establish what really matters and what an interruption would cost.

    Outputs Impact analysis · RTO and RPO targets · Risk register

  2. 02

    Plan

    A recovery and security strategy proportionate to risk, turned into usable plans and procedures.

    Outputs Recovery strategy · Plans and runbooks · Roadmap

  3. 03

    Test

    Tabletop exercises and technical tests to measure the real gap to your targets.

    Outputs Exercises · Restore tests · After-action report

  4. 04

    Improve

    Prioritized fixes, follow-up and plan updates until the next cycle.

    Outputs Remediation plan · Action tracking · Updated plans

Our principles

Vendor-neutral

We resell no products. Our recommendations serve your interest.

Proven by testing

A recovery capability only exists once it has been demonstrated.

Proportionate

Effort follows risk. No paperwork for its own sake.

Bilingual

We work in French and English, with teams across several countries.
