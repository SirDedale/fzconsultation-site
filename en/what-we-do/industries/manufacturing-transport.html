Industry

Manufacturing, transport and logistics

Keep producing and delivering when IT stops.

The context

Plants, warehouses and transport operations depend on tightly linked IT and operational systems. We help you understand those dependencies and prepare a realistic manual mode.

IT/OT boundary

An attack on IT can force production to stop.

Directory and privileged accounts

Active Directory is often the key to the whole infrastructure.

Operational catch-up

Schedules, crews and stock to recompute after the outage.

Key frameworks

  • NIS2
  • IEC 62443
  • ISO 22301
  • Cyber Essentials

Example engagements

These scenarios illustrate the kind of work we do in this sector. They do not describe real clients.

IT/OT mapping and manual mode

Situation
A manufacturer doesn't know which lines can run without IT.
What we do
Dependency mapping by line, manual procedures, on-site exercise.
Expected outcome
Documented degraded mode for each site.

Active Directory recovery plan

Situation
No procedure to rebuild the directory after a compromise.
What we do
Dedicated backups, isolated recovery forest, restore test.
Expected outcome
Tested recovery procedure and measured timing.

Transport operations continuity

Situation
A carrier wants to shorten catch-up time after an outage.
What we do
Scenario of losing scheduling systems, exercise and bottleneck analysis.
Expected outcome
Catch-up plan and recovery priorities.

Related case studies

  • Norsk Hydro: producing by hand rather than payingNorway, 2019. Ransomware hits the whole group; the company refuses to pay, switches to manual operations and communicates openly.
  • The CrowdStrike outage: when one update grounds the planesWorldwide, 2024. A faulty security software update crashes 8.5 million Windows devices. Delta takes five days to recover.
