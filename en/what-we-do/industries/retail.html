Industry

Retail

Open stores, online orders, payments: everything has to keep going.

The context

Retail combines stores, warehouses, e-commerce and payments in a tightly coupled system. We help protect the human entry points and prepare a credible degraded mode.

Social engineering

Help desks and providers targeted to reset access.

Coupled systems

Orders, stock and payments go down together.

Peak seasons

An outage during the holidays costs far more.

Key frameworks

  • PCI DSS
  • GDPR
  • Law 25 (Quebec)
  • NIST CSF 2.0

Example engagements

These scenarios illustrate the kind of work we do in this sector. They do not describe real clients.

Help desk review

Situation
A retailer wants to prevent a fraudulent-reset attack.
What we do
Review of identity checks, in-house and at the provider, scenario test.
Expected outcome
Strengthened procedures and agent training.

Store degraded mode

Situation
Stores can't sell without the central point-of-sale system.
What we do
Offline sales, stock and replenishment procedures.
Expected outcome
Store guide and regional exercise.

Pre-peak resilience

Situation
Leadership wants to be ready before the holidays.
What we do
Single-point-of-failure review, load and recovery tests, crisis exercise.
Expected outcome
Readiness plan and crisis team ready.
