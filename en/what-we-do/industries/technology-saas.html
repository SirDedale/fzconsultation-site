Technology and SaaS

Your availability is your product.

The context

Your customers send security questionnaires, ask for a SOC 2 report and, in finance, treat you as a critical ICT provider under DORA. We help you prove it without slowing your teams down.

Risky releases

One faulty update can hit every customer at once.

Single region

A regional cloud outage stops the service.

Customer requirements

Questionnaires, audits and availability clauses.

Example engagements

These scenarios illustrate the kind of work we do in this sector. They do not describe real clients.

SOC 2 readiness

Situation
A vendor needs its first SOC 2 Type II report.
What we do
Gap analysis, availability and continuity controls, evidence preparation.
Expected outcome
Control program ready for the auditor.

Multi-region recovery

Situation
The service runs in a single cloud region.
What we do
Design of recovery in a second region, failover tests.
Expected outcome
Tested architecture and failover runbooks.

Release safety

Situation
After the CrowdStrike outage, a customer demands guarantees on updates.
What we do
Review of staged rollouts, rollbacks and stop criteria.
Expected outcome
Release policy documented for customers.
