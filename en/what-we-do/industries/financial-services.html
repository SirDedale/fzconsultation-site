Sector

Financial services

High regulatory expectations, critical third parties everywhere and almost no tolerance for disruption.

Regulators no longer ask whether you have a plan. They ask whether you have proved you stay within your impact tolerance.

The context

Financial regulators now expect evidence: important services identified, impact tolerances set, scenarios tested and ICT providers under control. We help turn those requirements into verifiable capabilities.

Critical ICT providers

Cloud, core banking, payments: a third party's outage becomes yours within minutes, often before you even hear about it from anyone but your own customers. DORA and FCA/PRA rules now require proof of this, not just an assurance.

High-risk change

Poorly tested migrations and releases remain one of the most common causes of major incidents, as TSB's 2018 outage showed. A high-risk change needs a rollback plan as solid as the deployment plan itself.

Fraud and ransomware

Periods of disruption attract fraud attempts, because normal controls are often loosened in the rush to restore service. Planning for this risk before the incident happens avoids stacking a financial crisis on top of an operational one.

Key frameworks

  • DORA
  • FCA / PRA
  • BSIF / OSFI B-13 · E-21
  • SOC 2
  • ISO 22301
