Services

Ongoing advisory

The expertise of a resilience lead, without a full-time hire.

What we cover

For organizations that need regular follow-through: roadmap delivery, regulatory readiness and reporting to leadership.

Roadmap delivery

We track the actions on your roadmap, keep priorities current as risks evolve, and coordinate with your internal teams and suppliers. This regular follow-through, more than the initial plan, is usually what determines whether a roadmap actually gets delivered.

Regulatory readiness

We support you through DORA, NIS2, FCA/PRA or OSFI expectations, and SOC 2 audits, depending on the markets you operate in. This support includes preparing the evidence itself, not just interpreting the regulatory text.

Leadership reporting

We produce clear metrics for leadership and the board, showing how risk is actually evolving rather than a list of completed tasks. These reports also serve as a record if a regulator or client ever asks.

What you get

  • Quarterly resilience review
  • Risk and action dashboard
  • Support for audits and customer questionnaires

Frequently asked questions

How is ongoing advisory structured?

As a number of days per month, adjusted over time: more before an audit, lighter afterwards.
