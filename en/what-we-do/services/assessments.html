Services

Resilience and security assessments

Know where you stand, what matters most and where to start.

What we cover

A fixed-scope engagement, usually a few weeks depending on the size of the organization. A good starting point, ahead of an audit or after an incident.

  1. 1 to 2 weeksInterviews and discoveryLeadership, IT, operations, suppliers
  2. 1 weekAnalysisComparison to frameworks and your targets
  3. Half a dayReadoutFindings and roadmap for leadership

Interviews and discovery

We run grouped interviews with leadership, IT teams, operations and your key suppliers. This format keeps the time asked of each person low while still covering the whole organization.

Analysis

We compare your practices against the frameworks relevant to your sector, and against your own targets where they already exist. The gap between the two becomes the basis for the roadmap that follows.

Readout

We present the findings to leadership in business language, translated into risks and decisions rather than technical jargon. This is often what finally unlocks the budget or mandate needed to act.

What you get

  • Findings report and maturity score
  • Risk register
  • Prioritized roadmap
  • Executive presentation

Frequently asked questions

Does it take a lot of our teams' time?

No. We group interviews and reuse existing documentation to keep the effort on your teams low.
