Expertise

Cloud and infrastructure resilience

Architectures that tolerate the failure of a component, a zone or a provider.

What we cover

Major outages often come from a dependency nobody had identified. We review your architecture to find those weak points and propose fixes proportionate to the risk.

Architecture review

We review redundancy, zones and regions, networking, DNS and identity management across AWS, Azure or Google Cloud. These are often the real cause of an outage that looked, at first, like it was limited to a single application.

Shared responsibility

We clarify what your cloud provider actually covers and what remains your responsibility, a point often misunderstood between technical teams and leadership. That clarity avoids unpleasant surprises at the worst possible time.

SaaS data protection

We set up backup and recovery for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and your critical business applications. These platforms protect their own infrastructure, but rarely your data against accidental or malicious deletion.

Single points of failure

We identify the components, people or suppliers whose loss alone would be enough to stop an entire service. These single points of failure are often invisible until the day they cause the outage.

Monitoring and alerting

We set up monitoring that detects an outage before your customers do, with alerts that actually reach the right people, at any hour. An alert no one sees is worth no more than having no monitoring at all.

What you get

  • Architecture review and risk map
  • Prioritized list of single points of failure
  • Target architecture and transition plan
