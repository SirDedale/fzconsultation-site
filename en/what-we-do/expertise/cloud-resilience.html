Expertise

Cloud and infrastructure resilience

Architectures that tolerate the failure of a component, a zone or a provider.

What we cover

Major outages often come from a dependency nobody had identified. We review your architecture to find those weak points and propose fixes proportionate to the risk.

Architecture review

Redundancy, zones and regions, networking, DNS and identity across AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.

Shared responsibility

Clarify what the provider covers and what remains yours.

SaaS data protection

Backup and recovery for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and business applications.

Single points of failure

Identify components, people or suppliers whose loss would stop a service.

Monitoring and alerting

Detect outages before your customers do, with alerts that reach the right people.

What you get

  • Architecture review and risk map
  • Prioritized list of single points of failure
  • Target architecture and transition plan

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with a specific cloud provider?

No. We work with AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and on-premises or hybrid environments.
