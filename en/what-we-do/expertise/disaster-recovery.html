Expertise

Disaster recovery

Recovery that works on the day you need it, not just on paper.

What we cover

We work with you to set realistic RTO and RPO targets, then design, document and verify how to meet them, whether your systems are on-premises, in the cloud or hybrid.

RTO and RPO targets

We set targets per service, based on the real impact an interruption would have on your organization, not on industry averages that fit no one in particular. These targets then guide the whole recovery strategy.

Recovery architecture

We review or design your secondary sites, replication mechanisms and cloud recovery setup. The goal is an architecture that delivers on its promises the day it is actually called on, not just on a diagram.

Backup strategy

We design a backup strategy with isolated, immutable copies that resist the ransomware now specifically targeting backups. Every copy comes with a verified restore, not just a log that says it succeeded.

Recovery runbooks

We document precise steps, ordered by the real technical dependencies between your systems. They are written to be followed by a team under pressure, at three in the morning, not to impress an auditor.

Recovery testing

We run real, timed restores and failovers, to measure the gap between your targets and what actually happens. Every test ends with a list of concrete fixes, not just a finding.

What you get

  • RTO and RPO targets signed off by leadership
  • Recovery strategy and architecture
  • Step-by-step recovery runbooks
  • Test report and remediation plan

Frequently asked questions

Aren't our backups enough?

Backups are necessary but not sufficient. You also need to know the restore order, how long it really takes, and that the copies have not been compromised.

Isn't being in the cloud already disaster recovery?

Not automatically. The provider protects its infrastructure, but your data, configuration and access remain your responsibility.
