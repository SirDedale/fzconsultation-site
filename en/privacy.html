Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

The data controller is FZ Consultation. For any question about your personal information, email contact@fzconsultation.xyz. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is: Fabien Mariou, contact@fzconsultation.xyz.

Information we collect

  • Messages you send us. Name, email, organization and message content, when you email us or use the form. We use them only to reply and follow up on your request.
  • Contact form. The form is delivered by Formspree, which forwards your message to us.
  • Booking. If you book a call, the booking tool processes your name, email and chosen time.
  • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures traffic in aggregate, without cookies or individual profiles.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Self-assessment. Your answers are processed only in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • AI vision questionnaire. Your answers stay in your browser. They only reach us if you choose to email us the summary.
  • Display preferences. Your language and theme choice (light, dark or automatic) is kept in your browser's local storage and never transmitted. We use no cookies.
  • Search and calculator. Search uses a static index and runs in your browser: your queries are not transmitted. Numbers entered in the calculator never leave your browser.

Legal bases

We process your information on the basis of your consent or our legitimate interest in responding to requests and keeping the site secure.

Retention

We keep exchanges with prospective clients for up to three years after the last contact, then delete them, unless an ongoing business relationship or a legal obligation requires longer retention.

Service providers and transfers outside your country

Some providers (hosting and, where applicable, form, booking or analytics) are located in the United States. These transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where it applies. We never sell or rent your information.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (GDPR, UK GDPR, Quebec's Law 25, Canada's PIPEDA or others), you can request access to your information, correction, deletion or portability, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw your consent. Email contact@fzconsultation.xyz; we respond within 30 days.

Complaints

You can also contact a supervisory authority: the CNIL in France, the ICO in the UK, Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the authority in your country.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS and sets no cookies. Access to messages we receive is limited to those who need it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.
