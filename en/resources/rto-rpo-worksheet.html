RTO / RPO worksheet

Start from what the business can tolerate, not from the technology: the right target is set with business owners.

How to use it

Fill in one row per important business service. Ask its owner: "After how long a stoppage does the damage become unacceptable? How much recent data can we afford to lose?" Then compare with what your systems can really do, measured in a test, not read in a brochure.

Download (Word, .docx)

Definitions

RTO
Recovery Time Objective: the target maximum time between the interruption and the service coming back.
RPO
Recovery Point Objective: the maximum age of restored data, hence the data loss accepted.
MTPD
Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption: beyond it, the viability of the activity is threatened. The RTO must stay well below it.

Tips

Tier your targets

Four tiers (for example critical, important, useful, deferrable) are enough. A different RTO for every system is impossible to test and to fund.

Measure, don't guess

An empty "tested" column is an answer in itself: it shows the objective has never been demonstrated.

Follow dependencies

A service cannot recover faster than the slowest of its parts: identity, DNS, network, suppliers.

Review yearly

A new application, a supplier change or a merger shifts the targets. Review them at least once a year.

General-purpose template provided as a starting point. Adapt it to your organization and have it reviewed by your legal, compliance and security advisers before use. It is not legal advice.
