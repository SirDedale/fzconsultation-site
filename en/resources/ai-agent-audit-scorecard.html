AI agent audit scorecard

A lightweight version of the FZ AI Auditor method, so your team can take a first inventory and repeat it.

How to use it

Fill in one row per deployed agent or automation. Back every column with evidence (logs, traces, invoices, reviewed samples) rather than the design document. The ratings below are an example: adapt the thresholds to your risk tolerance. Re-run the table every quarter and whenever a model, prompt or permission changes.

Download (Word, .docx)

Blast-radius rating grid (example)

Low
Reversible errors, few cases touched, detection the same day.
Moderate
Reversible but numerous errors, or detection within days. Costly to correct but not critical.
High
Partly irreversible errors, or errors touching customers, money or regulatory obligations, with slow detection.
Critical
Irreversible errors, or errors affecting safety, large sums or compliance, at high volume, with uncertain detection. Requires human approval, staged rollout and a tested kill switch.

Tips

Start with the inventory

Include agents teams adopted without going through IT.

Measure value

Replace estimates with real costs and a hand-reviewed sample.

Rate the worst case

Rate blast radius by what can go wrong at real volume, not by the average.

Match controls to the rating

Record the gap between existing controls and those the rating requires.

General-purpose template provided as a starting point. Adapt it to your organization and have it reviewed by your legal, compliance and security advisers before use. It is not legal advice.
